METHODOLOGY
1. Identify the public insurance dataset and define charges as the continuous target.
2. Load the CSV in R and inspect structure, types, missing values and duplicates.
3. Clean category strings and convert categorical predictors to factors.
4. Perform descriptive statistics and exploratory visualizations.
5. Test the difference in mean charges by smoker status using Welch's t-test.
6. Calculate Pearson correlations for numeric variables.
7. Split records into training and testing sets.
8. Fit multiple linear regression on the training data.
9. Evaluate using MAE, RMSE and R-squared; run five-fold cross-validation.
10. Inspect residual and regression diagnostic plots.
11. Document findings, limitations and future improvements.
